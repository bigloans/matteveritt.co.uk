Changelog

Each release of this site, newest first.

  1. Version 7

    12 November 2026

    A new CV, figures measured again from the spec, and more product evidence. The case study adds cost, time to market, stalled pilots and a roadmap to 1.0. The product read adds discovery and a weekly report, and a new short read shows how I rank the backlog.

  2. Version 6

    12 November 2026

    A new short read, Managing the spec as a product, covers the product decisions behind the spec. The skills now link to it.

  3. Version 5

    12 November 2026

    A reduce motion button in the header that remembers your choice. Figures show their real value instead of counting up, the skills link to the work that shows them, the case study opens with a short summary and the adoption figures are up to date.

  4. Version 4

    14 October 2026

    The gradients and motion are back, with the aurora now running behind the whole page. The version 3 content, header and gate demo stay, and the bug demo now animates the bug's route through the harness.

  5. Version 3

    14 October 2026

    A calmer site. Decorative motion is gone, colour tokens are now primitives and roles, and two demos let you try the gate and follow a bug through the agent harness.

  6. Version 2

    13 October 2026

    The single case study became a portfolio, with a landing page, the case study, two short reads and this site's spec, all built from one token file.

  7. Version 1

    9 October 2026

    Launched as a single page with one case study, on a design system built for AI coding agents.
