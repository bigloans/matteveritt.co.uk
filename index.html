About

I'm a design operations manager in Nottingham. I started as a junior UI designer in March 2020, and I now run the design system for a portfolio of 60+ products.

From 2020 to 2026 I built and ran our first design system, a 45-component library in React, Vue and Angular. I led a front-end engineer and a UI designer on it and took its roadmap to CTO level.

In July 2026 I replaced it with a specification that AI coding agents build from. I own its strategy, roadmap, backlog, releases and adoption, and colleagues come to me for anything concerning the design system.
